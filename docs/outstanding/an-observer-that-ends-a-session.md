An observer's `session/close` or disconnect can end a session that has no client left that may drive it.

- Area: cortex-core
- Owner: the owner; before step 27

| Item | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| **An observer keeps a session running, and closing its own view can then end it.** A session ends when the last client that has it open closes it or disconnects, whatever that client's role. So when the only `user` client of a session leaves, an `observer` still watching keeps the session running and holding its lock, with nobody to drive it; and the observer's `session/close`, or its disconnect, then ends the session. "Everything that starts, changes or ends something is refused" for an observer ([../architecture/11-rpc-protocol.md](../architecture/11-rpc-protocol.md)), and closing its own view is allowed, so the two readings meet here. The choices: leave it (an observer that stays is what keeps a session alive for it to watch); end a session when its last client that may drive leaves, and tell the observers; or keep a session alive with observers only until a time limit. Found by the independent review of step 13a. **Built in step 27 as recommended, pending the owner's word** ([../steps/27/answered.md](../steps/27/answered.md), "The open items that name step 27"): an observer's `session/close` or disconnect never ends a session; only a client that may drive it (a `user` or a `supervisor`) ends one, when no other client has it open, and with `keep_sessions` (`serve --socket`) no disconnect does. A session whose last driver left while an observer watches goes on for the observer, and on after it; outside `--socket` an observer is never the last client of a session (`cortex-core`'s `closing` tests). The owner may still choose the second or third choice. | The owner | Before step 27 is merged |
