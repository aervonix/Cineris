| **Budgets for searching, diffing and JSON decoding on real code** (the eighth tooling step measured them; nothing is set). Measured at the desk in a release build with other sessions' runs beside it ([steps/tooling-8/answered.md](../steps/tooling-8/answered.md)): a search of the 20,162-file corpus 16 to 21 ms at the median (33 to 38 ms on 4 cores); a tool's re-read of a changed real file 0.33 to 0.40 ms at the median, 0.8 to 1.0 ms at the 95th percentile, about 0.15 ms of it the diff; a 1 MB tool call of real code decoded in 7.7 ms, which is 282 MB/s of the stream the decoder reads (2.18 MB of server-sent events, the call's text escaped as JSON in 200-byte pieces) and 130 MB/s of the call's own 1 MB. On the hosted runners (`try` 43852836180, `perf-ab` against `main`, three rounds, the median and the range): a search of the corpus 101 to 110 ms on Linux and 316 to 382 ms on Windows (272 to 432 ms over the rounds); a re-read of a changed file 0.57 ms at the median and 1.5 ms at the 95th on Linux, 1.16 and 2.7 ms on Windows, of which an unchanged re-read is 0.33 and 0.75 ms; the 1 MB call decoded in 15.7 ms on Linux and 18.0 ms on Windows (about 139 and 121 MB/s of its 2.18 MB stream; 64 and 56 MB/s of the call's 1 MB); the index's warm pass 72 and 108 ms, its cold build 3.5 and 5.0 s. Windows' search is near the runner figure proposed below, and the owner may want it at 500 ms.
