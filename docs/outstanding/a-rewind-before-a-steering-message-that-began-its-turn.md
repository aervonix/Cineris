A rewind or fork `before` a steering message that began a turn of its own leaves that turn's first entries on the branch, and the next `/undo` undoes the turn before.

- Area: cineris-core
- Owner: step 32

| Item | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| **A steering message that began its turn is taken for steering inside one.** `turn_start` in `crates/cineris-core/src/rewind.rs` (lines 129-151 at the review's commit, `77ffbbcc`) takes every user message with `delivery: steer` for steering that came into a turn already running, and goes back to just before its own entry. But a steering message that finds no boundary to join starts a turn of its own and keeps `steer` (`cineris-agent`'s `turn.rs`, `next_turn`; [11-rpc-protocol.md](../architecture/11-rpc-protocol.md) says so of `turn/steer`). **The scenario:** turn `t_2` records a plan notice `i_3`, then the steering message as its prompt; a rewind `before` that message records `t_2` as rewound but leaves `i_3` on the branch; the next `/undo` undoes only `i_3`, turn 1 stays, the log records `t_2` undone twice, and the interface says "The last turn is undone". A fork `before` such a message has the same twin. In the interface, `points_read` skips every steering item, so such a turn is never a point, and every later `turn N` is off by one: the picker, the question, the notice and the viewer's title. Found by the review of step 25 and confirmed by a scratch test it ran against `cineris-core` (`review_before_a_steering_message_that_began_its_turn_leaves_the_turns_first_entries`, kept with the review, not in the repository): the branch read `[i_1, i_2, i_3]` after the rewind and `[i_1, i_2]` after the undo, with `t_2` undone twice. It is the first finding of the review of step 25's fixes again, for the one kind of prompt that fix missed. **The docs are the specification here, and the code differs from them:** `before`'s docs (`cineris-protocol`'s `methods.rs`, [11-rpc-protocol.md](../architecture/11-rpc-protocol.md), [09-tui.md](../architecture/09-tui.md) "The points", and `rewind.rs`'s module doc) say a rewind `before` a prompt goes back to before its turn, which does not hold for such a message. **Recommendation:** in `turn_start`, a user message is its turn's prompt when no earlier entry of its turn is a user message or a response, whatever its `delivery`; that changes neither the protocol nor the log. Counting such turns as points in the interface needs each item's turn on the wire, a protocol addition; without it, 09 should say they are not points. | Step 32 (hardening) | Before the first release |
