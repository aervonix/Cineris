| **The policy's guards of a stop from a call's run have no test of their own.** Since the stop-ends-turn step, a call whose run stopped its turn marks its result as the stop (`ToolResult::stopping_turn`), and the round ends at that call ([03-agent-loop.md](../architecture/03-agent-loop.md), "A stop from a call's run"; [steps/stop-ends-turn/answered.md](../steps/stop-ends-turn/answered.md)). The policy's guards stay, as a second line: `EnginePolicy::stop_turn`'s narrowing (so a call not yet started is decided again), the stopped turn's `Decision::Stop`, `StopsWithTurn`'s refusal, and the `stop_turn` calls of `CallHost::decide` and `ext::exec::look_after`. Their five breaks are marked `expect: survives`: step 28's `a-stopped-host-call-leaves-the-turn-going` and `exec-protected-change-does-not-stop-the-turn`, step 12's `a-stop-of-the-turn-moves-no-count`, and step 26's `a-stopped-turn-goes-on` and `a-stopped-turn-runs-another-command`. **The window they guard is real:** a confined command found to only read runs beside other calls that only read, and another such call can start after the command's run called `stop_turn` and before the round has taken the command's result; only the policy's guards keep it from running then. No test can hold a command in that window today. **What would close it:** a point to wait at inside `RecordingShell`, after the stop and before the result is returned, and a core test that holds a read-only command there while a second read-only command is free to start, and sees it refused; then the five breaks name that test again. Security fix 3's rewrite of `RecordingShell::run_by` exports awaited points (`cineris_base::hook::await_point!`) from `cineris_core`: `BEFORE_THE_COMMAND_STARTS` and `BEFORE_THE_LOOK_AFTER`, and `AFTER_THE_TURN_IS_STOPPED`, at the end of `run_by`, reached only by a command that stopped its turn, which is the one the test waits at. The two breaks of step 28 are of the extension's side, where nothing is decided after the stop today: the engine ends a script's call at its first stop, so it makes no host call after it, and an extension's tool runs alone.
