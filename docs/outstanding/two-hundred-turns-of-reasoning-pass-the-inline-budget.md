Where the rest is, by reading: (1) **the session holds each reasoning twice**: the assistant item's `ReasoningPart` has the text and its `raw` block, and the Anthropic decoder writes the whole thinking text into that block too (`anthropic/decode.rs`, `Shape::Thinking`: `"thinking": <text>`, `"signature"`), so 200 × 16 KiB is 6.4 MB in the session's items, and again in what the context holds of them where it keeps its own; (2) **each request carries every earlier reasoning**, replayed with its signature to the model that wrote it, so the request body at turn 200 is over 3 MB of JSON, made twice a turn (the call and the answer), with the messages built for it beside the items: transient, but each is a peak (the peaks are 22 MB over `main`'s), and the shipped build's allocator (musl's) keeps much of what a peak took; (3) the session log's writer and the event stream's snapshot of an item in progress hold a reasoning only while it streams. The scripted server is the test's own process, not the binary's. **The same tree with no reasoning** (`CINERIS_PERF_REASONING=0`, on an otherwise idle machine, the same day) holds inline 32.3 MB after the turns and 41.1 at the peak, as `main` does: so the 16.1 MB resident and the 22.9 MB of peak are the reasoning's, and of them at most 1.6 MB is the interface's.
