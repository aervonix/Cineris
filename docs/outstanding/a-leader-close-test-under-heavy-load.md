| **The test of a leader's terminal closing died once in a hundred under heavy synthetic load.** `cortex-tui`'s `signals::sys_closing_the_terminal_of_a_leader_ends_as_sighup` (the application, as its terminal's session leader, has the terminal's other end closed under it) passed 99 of 100 runs of `cargo xtask stress -p cortex-tui -p cortex-term --count 100 --cores 2 --load 6 -E 'test(/signals::|handoff::/)'` at the desk after the fixes of step 23's review; it passes in every plain and loaded run of the whole crates. In the one failure the child wrote its first frame's mark and nothing more, and died within 5 s, before the diagnostic thread the test now starts could say where it waited. The likely cause, not confirmed: the 3 s watchdog of the hand-over (`alarm(3)`) firing in a child starved by the load, whose debug build's end took longer than 3 s from the signal; the watchdog is as specified and was not weakened for the test. Recommendation: have the test's child note the watchdog's firing (a SIGALRM handler of the test's own that writes to its report before it ends the process), run the targeted stress again, and if it is the watchdog, give the child under load a longer start or count it a machine stall. | The next tooling step, or step 32 | Before the first release |
