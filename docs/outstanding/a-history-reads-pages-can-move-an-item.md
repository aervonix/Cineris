| **A history read's pages can move an item between them when parallel items end out of order.** `session/read` pages by an offset into the log followed by the items in progress, and the application's read (step 23's review fixes) keeps for each item in progress the `seq` its page gave. When several items are in progress at once (parallel tool results) and a page boundary falls between them, one that completes before an earlier one moves into the log at an earlier index: the next page then gives an item already drawn in progress again, and the one that completed is never drawn, its events up to that page's `seq` dropped as having come to nothing. Found by reading in the review of step 23's fixes; it needs more than 500 items before the boundary, so no test reaches it yet.
