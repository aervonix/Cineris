| **The application chooses a prompt's delivery from its own view of whether a turn runs, which lags the core's.** Three ways, each found by step 23's review: (1) prompts held while the session opened are sent at once, the first as a prompt and the rest as `queue`; when the core refuses the first (`history_changing`, say), the second, queued with no turn running, starts a turn of its own, and the first comes back into the editor behind it, so their order is turned round.
